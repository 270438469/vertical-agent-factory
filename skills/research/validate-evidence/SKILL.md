# Validate Evidence

## Procedure

1. Inspect result status, facts and evidence.
2. Reject successful factual output without a source reference.
3. Mark a valid result as validated without changing its claims.

## Failure handling

Fail closed when the result contract or evidence invariant is violated.

## Completion criteria

The result metadata records successful validation.
